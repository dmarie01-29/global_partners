# global_partners
To Create a unified data view that provides business insights for Global Partners in understanding customer behavior, spending patterns, and overall business performance across all restaurant locations and platforms

Project Overview
Objective: We need a unified data view that helps us understand customer behavior, spending patterns, and overall business performance across all restaurant locations and platforms. 
The system should calculate and update Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) daily, classify customers into high, medium, and low value groups, and segment them using recency, frequency, and monetary (RFM) metrics. It should identify customers at risk of churn, highlight sales trends and 
seasonal patterns, and compare the performance of loyalty members versus non-members. 
Additionally, we need insights into the best- and worst-performing locations and the impact of discounts or promotions on revenue and profitability. All these insights should be accessible through an interactive dashboard to support data-driven marketing, operational, and strategic decisions.
